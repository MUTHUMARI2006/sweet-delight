Since 1984

A Symphony of Spices, Ghee, and Devotion

Sweet Delight began as a quaint mithai shop with a single copper vat and a grandmother's sacred recipes for Mysore Pak, melt-in-the-mouth Palkova, and saffron-bathed Gulab Jamuns.

Four decades later, we still honor the slow, unhurried methods of our ancestors. From sourcing whole Goan cashews for our paper-thin Kaju Katli to hand-kneading fresh cow milk chhena for juicy Rasgullas, we celebrate India's sweetest heritage with every box.
